We demonstrate that this metric effectively proxies true performance, yielding a robust methodology for monitoring learning progress in complex settings. Finally, we complement these empirical findings with the first finite-time theoretical analysis of an anytime MOMAB Pareto Set Identification algorithm.

TempShap: When and How Much a Feature Matters in Time-Series Forecasting

TempShap：时序预测中特征何时及如何发挥作用的影响度量

Attribution for a time-series forecaster should answer two questions that a feature-level scalar cannot: when in the input window a feature acts, and how much its influence changes across the forecast horizon. Most existing Shapley explainers treat each feature as one player and therefore collapse both axes. We propose TempShap, a retrospective, loss-based Shapley framework whose players are the individual input cells $(t,i)$. Its attribution tensor yields temporal-localization and horizon-impact profiles. TempShap therefore makes fine-grained Shapley attribution both practical and operationally useful.

Provably Efficient Q-learning for Robust Reinforcement Learning

可证明高效的Q学习在稳健强化学习中的应用

Standard reinforcement learning (RL) algorithms based on Markov Decision Processes (MDPs) are vulnerable to sim-to-real gaps. To address this issue, distributionally robust RL algorithms based on robust MDPs (RMDPs) have been proposed, for ensuring that agents can perform reliably to unpredictable conditions in real-world environments. Despite of the rich literature, \revision{an efficient model-free method is still lacking} for robust RL, particularly for Markovian data, which is more relevant in practical applications. In this work, we take a step towards settling this problem by proposing a sample efficient (asynchronous Q-learning) algorithm for robust RL. In particular, consider a $\gamma$-discounted infinite-horizon RMDP with state space $\mathcal{S}$ and action space $\mathcal{A}$, where the uncertainty set is specified by the total variation (TV) distance with uncertainty level $\sigma$. Assuming access to a single trajectory of Markovian samples induced by a behavior policy and the nominal transition kernel, the sample complexity of the proposed Q-learning algorithm --- to achieve an entrywise $\varepsilon$-approximation of the optimal Q-function --- is bounded by $$ \frac{1}{\mu_{\mathsf{min}}\varepsilon^2(1-\gamma)^3\max\{1-\gamma,\sigma\}} + \frac{t_{\mathsf{mix}}}{\mu_{\mathsf{min}}(1-\gamma)} $$ up to logarithmic factors. Here, $t_{\mathsf{mix}}$ and $\mu_{\mathsf{min}}$ denote the mixing time and the minimum state-action occupancy probability of the sample trajectory, respectively. This result is nearly minimax optimal, with an additional factor of $\frac{1}{1-\gamma}$. Moreover, it achieves a significant improvement over existing bounds.

核心方法： Proposes a sample-efficient Q-learning algorithm for robust reinforcement learning (RL) in the context of robust Markov Decision Processes (RMDPs). The algorithm addresses uncertainties in real-world environments by using total variation distance to model the uncertainty set and leverages a single trajectory of Markovian samples.
数据来源： Single trajectory of Markovian samples induced by a behavior policy and the nominal transition kernel.
Alpha 潜力：

Combinatorial Arbitrage in Prediction Markets

预测市场中的组合套利策略

We develop a framework to identify and quantify arbitrage across logically related prediction-market contracts. We prove that prices outside the convex hull of admissible payoff vectors admit arbitrage and identify optimal arbitrage portfolios through integer programming. Using venue metadata and semantic analysis of resolution criteria, we recover 1.9 million logical relations and compile them into constraint systems covering more than 255,000 contracts. We replay full-depth Polymarket order books to simulate an arbitrageur executing these portfolios against resting orders. Among settled portfolios, an ideal arbitrageur that takes the full displayed depth of every opportunity and reuses its capital at resolution earns a risk-free return of 3.5% a week after spreads and fees. We find that arbitrage opportunities persist longer and attract less observed participation where relation discovery and execution are more costly.

核心方法： The paper presents a framework for identifying and quantifying arbitrage opportunities in prediction markets by using integer programming to determine optimal arbitrage portfolios. It employs venue metadata and semantic analysis to recover logical relations between contracts.

深度灾难风险感知强化学习

Estimating extreme risk in reinforcement learning becomes increasingly difficult as the confidence level increases, since only a small number of observations contribute to empirical estimates of Conditional Value-at-Risk (CVaR). In this paper, we extend an Extreme Value Theory (EVT) approach for CVaR optimization to high-dimensional neural-network policies. Using a peaks-over-threshold representation and method-of-moments estimation, we obtain a differentiable tail-risk objective and show that, conditional on the selected threshold, its finite-sample gradient can be computed directly through automatic differentiation. Our results show similar hedging behavior for EVT- and empirical-CVaR-trained policies under normal conditions, while EVT-trained policies exhibit lower hedging losses under the extreme-scenario evaluation across the training sample sizes considered. These results suggest that EVT can provide a practical alternative for training neural policies when the observations relevant to extreme risk are scarce.

Through controlled comparisons across eleven datasets and three backbones, we show that informative channel assignments improve forecasting beyond matched shared capacity and random allocation. In matched comparisons, PCR outperforms competing specialization methods on average while requiring substantially fewer trainable parameters.

Dynamic Multi-Step Decoding for Time-Series Foundation Models

时间序列基础模型的动态多步解码

Time-series forecasting models typically use a fixed decoding strategy at inference time: either one-shot prediction of the full horizon or autoregressive rollout over atomic chunks. While one-shot decoding reduces error accumulation, it can discard useful sequential structure; conversely, autoregressive decoding preserves temporal dependencies but may compound errors. We argue that the optimal decoding strategy is not fixed across inputs, but should be selected adaptively for each forecasting instance. We propose a dynamic strategy selector that treats the forecast rollout schedule as an inference-time decision problem for frozen pretrained time-series foundation models. The selector is a learned network that ranks candidate rollout schedules using hidden states of the backbone, and is trained on historical windows of the evaluated tasks for each backbone. To make the candidate set tractable over the exponentially large space of horizon partitions, we use dynamic programming that reduces candidate evaluation to quadratic complexity while preserving much of the oracle gain. At deployment, five independently trained selectors vote on each decision, and the policy follows their consensus, falling back to the backbone's one-shot forecast where they disagree. The construction requires only the backbone's hidden states and lightweight selectors, with no fine-tuning of the forecasting backbone. On fev-bench with a frozen Chronos-2 backbone, agreement-gated selection improves point-forecast skill (MASE) over one-shot decoding with statistical significance, mostly on longer horizons. It recovers about 6-8% of the gain that the same dynamic program attains with ground-truth selection. With a frozen Moirai-1.1-R-small backbone, the improvement is also significant on the full benchmark, and for both backbones we claim gains in point accuracy only.

核心方法： The paper introduces a dynamic multi-step decoding strategy for time-series forecasting models, which adaptively selects between one-shot prediction and autoregressive decoding based on input characteristics. The approach uses a learned network to rank candidate rollout schedules and employs dynamic programming for efficient evaluation. The method is applied to frozen pretrained time-series foundation models without fine-tuning the backbone.
数据来源： The paper evaluates the method on the fev-bench dataset using frozen Chronos-2 and Moirai-1.1-R-small backbones, suggesting applicability to general time-series data, including financial time-series.

LEAP: a Latent Embedding Anomaly Patch-Transformer for time-series anomaly detection

LEAP：用于时间序列异常检测的潜在嵌入异常补丁Transformer

Time-series anomaly detection is dominated by reconstruction-based neural detectors, whose objective is structurally flawed. Since the target is also the input, an expressive model can learn to reconstruct anomalies too, leaving uninformative residuals. Recent large-scale benchmarks show that, on curated data, elaborate deep detectors rarely beat simple statistical baselines, while current leaders rely on representation learning. We propose LEAP (Latent Embedding Anomaly Patch-Transformer), which combines masked prediction with latent representation-based anomaly evidence, without relying on reconstruction or attention-based scoring. The model removes patches from a window and predicts them from the remaining temporal and cross-channel context, so prediction errors measure how well learned dynamics explain hidden segments. Deterministic complementary group masking ensures every time step is scored while hidden, with identical visible fractions during training and inference. LEAP adds three label-free evidence sources: latent-embedding novelty calibrated by local density, local temporal contrast, and per-channel novelty pooled over channels selected by six label-free criteria. Under the TSB-AD protocol, the full model achieves a VUS-PR of $0.5179 \pm 0.0022$ on $180$ held-out multivariate series. On TSB-AD-U, a univariate variant combining multi-scale masked-prediction scoring with latent novelty, without contrast or channel-pool novelty, achieves $0.6194 \pm 0.0016$ on $350$ held-out series. Both results average three random seeds. A five-stage ablation on TSB-AD-M, supported by paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests, shows statistically significant gains from each successive component.

核心方法： LEAP combines masked prediction with latent representation-based anomaly evidence, avoiding reconstruction-based methods. It uses deterministic complementary group masking to predict hidden segments from temporal and cross-channel contexts, supplemented by three label-free evidence sources: latent-embedding novelty, local temporal contrast, and per-channel novelty pooling.
数据来源： Evaluated on multivariate (180 series) and univariate (350 series) time-series datasets under the TSB-AD protocol.

LLM-Enhanced Heterogeneous Graph Learning with Business Semantics for Financial Fraud Detection

基于商业语义的LLM增强异质图学习在金融欺诈检测中的应用

Financial fraud detection increasingly relies on heterogeneous transaction graphs, in which entities such as cards and merchants play distinct business roles and the risk meaning of an attribute depends on the entity's role: a high transaction volume may be routine for a merchant but unusual for a card. Heterogeneous graph neural networks preserve typed entities and relations but typically treat these attributes as role-agnostic inputs and leave their business meaning implicit. Meanwhile, most existing methods integrating large language models (LLMs) with graphs focus on homogeneous graphs or heterogeneous graphs with naturally available textual attributes, leaving their use on transaction graphs with structured attributes underexplored. To address this issue, we propose SemHGT, an LLM-enhanced framework that interprets structured attributes according to entity roles and makes the business semantics explicit in three stages: (1) role-aware prompts elicit risk-related business descriptions from node attributes, and a text encoder maps them to semantic vectors that enrich node representations; (2) semantic similarity guides multi-round sampling of target-specific path instances under transaction-to-transaction schemas; (3) schema-level tokens that combine local path evidence with global neighbor summaries are processed together with the target transaction by a unified transformer. Across three benchmark datasets, SemHGT leads the evaluated baselines on most dataset--metric comparisons. Further analyses provide additional evidence for the effectiveness of SemHGT's design and clarify how its semantic modeling and evidence selection contribute to fraud detection. Code is available at https://anonymous.4open.science/r/SemHGT-528A/.

核心方法： LLM-enhanced heterogeneous graph learning with explicit business semantics for financial fraud detection. The framework (SemHGT) incorporates role-aware prompts to generate risk-related business descriptions from node attributes, uses semantic similarity for path sampling, and processes schema-level tokens with a transformer.
数据来源： Transaction graphs with structured attributes (e.g., card and merchant entities) from benchmark datasets for financial fraud detection.
