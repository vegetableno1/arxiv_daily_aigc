A Controlled Comparison of Deep Learning Architectures for Multi-Horizon Financial Forecasting: Evidence from 918 Experiments

深度学习架构在多时间范围金融预测中的受控比较：基于918次实验的证据

Reliable comparison of deep learning architectures for financial forecasting requires controlled evaluation that separates architectural effects from hyperparameter tuning, random initialization, forecast horizon, and asset characteristics. We present a five-stage benchmark using equal-budget Bayesian hyperparameter optimization, category-level configuration freezing, three-seed final training, held-out metric aggregation, and rank-based statistical validation. Nine architectures from Transformer, MLP/linear, convolutional, and recurrent families are evaluated with direct multi-step prediction from five OHLCV features across 12 assets (four cryptocurrency, four foreign exchange, four equity-index instruments), using chronological 70/15/15 splits of 30,000 hourly windows per asset at 4- and 24-hour horizons. The benchmark includes 918 runs (270 optimization trials, 648 final training runs). Across 24 asset-horizon points, ModernTCN has mean rank 1.333 and the lowest RMSE in 18 points (75.0%), followed by PatchTST (mean rank 2.000). Global ranking differences are strongly supported by an Iman-Davenport test (F(8,184)=101.36, p<1e-15), with 33 of 36 Holm-adjusted pairwise comparisons significant. Rankings are consistent across horizons for all 12 assets (rho>=0.683; Stouffer p=3.47e-10), while errors increase at the longer horizon. Architecture explains 99.90% of raw error variance and 68.33% after within-slot normalization excluding LSTM; seed variance contributes at most 0.04% (0.02% without LSTM). Mean directional accuracy is 50.08%. These findings provide a statistically validated comparison of nine architectures, identify ModernTCN and PatchTST as leading performers under the evaluated conditions, and offer a reproducible benchmark for multi-asset financial time-series forecasting.

核心方法： The paper presents a controlled evaluation framework for comparing deep learning architectures in financial forecasting, utilizing a five-stage benchmark with equal-budget Bayesian hyperparameter optimization, category-level configuration freezing, three-seed final training, held-out metric aggregation, and rank-based statistical validation. It evaluates nine architectures from Transformer, MLP/linear, convolutional, and recurrent families across 12 assets with multi-horizon predictions.
数据来源： The study uses OHLCV (Open, High, Low, Close, Volume) data from four cryptocurrency pairs, four foreign exchange instruments, and four equity-index instruments, totaling 30,000 hourly windows per asset with chronological 70/15/15 splits.
Alpha 潜力：

The identified top-performing architectures (ModernTCN and PatchTST) demonstrate strong predictive accuracy and consistency across different assets and forecast horizons, suggesting potential for developing robust algorithmic trading strategies. The methodology's rigor and reproducibility enhance its practical applicability.

Admissible Portfolio Optimization: Information Constraints, Conditional Efficient Frontiers and the Price of Causal Identification

可接受的投资组合优化：信息约束、条件有效前沿与因果识别的代价

Mean--variance portfolio choice takes the conditioning information as given, so that two errors about it, using variables not available at the decision time and treating common variation as idiosyncratic, pass into the portfolio unseen. This paper turns what that practice assumes into a condition declared before estimation, certified from data and priced when it fails. The conditioning information becomes a decision variable subject to admissibility constraints on availability, arbitrage, statistical separation and, for interventional claims, invariance across regimes; an order in which no risk--return quantity enters selects an information class, and the classical problem is solved inside it. We prove that solutions exist, that the value of information is monotone only under an embedding that portfolio constraints can break, so that selection by decision loss admits look-ahead information whenever offered, that the two-stage solution and the joint envelope are minimal elements of two orders on one set, that the diversifiable part of risk is a property of the admissible class, and that causal identification has an explicit price. Allocation performance is downstream of the framework, since the information decision enters the decision regret at second order and the reported risk at first order, while the weights themselves move at first order. On market data the condition holds on small sets of individual securities and fails on pre-diversified portfolios and on books of realistic size, where a factor model with diagonal residual understates its portfolio's variance by a factor that the consequence statistic announces in advance; selecting the same information by decision loss yields portfolios that promise a fraction of the risk they realize.

核心方法： The paper introduces an admissible portfolio optimization framework that explicitly models conditioning information constraints, including temporal availability, arbitrage constraints, statistical separation, and causal invariance. It develops a formal approach to information selection before portfolio construction, proving solutions exist and analyzing information value under portfolio constraints.
数据来源： Implied to use standard market data (individual securities and pre-diversified portfolios), though not explicitly stated. Methodology is general enough to apply to any asset pricing dataset.
Alpha 潜力：

Addresses a fundamental source of model risk in portfolio optimization by properly accounting for information timing and causal structure. The framework could: 1) Identify information sets that minimize decision regret 2) Price the cost of causal misidentification 3) Correct systematic underestimation of portfolio risk in factor models

Tracking High-Frequency Trading Activity in China and Its Effects on Market Quality

追踪中国高频交易活动及其对市场质量的影响

We develop a new order-level proxy for high-frequency trading (HFT) activity based on precise order placement and study its effects on market quality. With quotes updating multiple times per second, millisecond-level order-book access makes high-frequency traders more likely to submit active exact-match orders, defined as limit orders priced exactly at the prevailing best opposite-side quote. Using Shenzhen Stock Exchange data, we validate the proxy: active exact-match orders respond faster to quote updates, cancel more rapidly, concentrate when depth imbalance is favorable and spreads are narrow, and predict mid-quote returns from 100 milliseconds through ten minutes. A difference-in-differences design exploiting cross-channel variation in order dissemination latency at the market open shows that lower latency increases active exact-match shares. This variation yields plausibly causal evidence that latency-induced HFT raises short-horizon price variation, wide-spread incidence, and adverse-selection exposure, while realized spreads decline and pricing error falls modestly. Unlike developed markets, China's T+1 constraint, absence of maker-taker rebates, and limits on rapid quote revision tilt speed advantages toward directional liquidity taking, highlighting how market design shapes the channel through which speed affects market quality.

核心方法： The paper develops a new order-level proxy for high-frequency trading (HFT) activity based on precise order placement, specifically focusing on active exact-match orders. The methodology includes validation through responsiveness to quote updates, cancellation speed, and predictive power for mid-quote returns. A difference-in-differences design is used to exploit cross-channel variation in order dissemination latency.
数据来源： Shenzhen Stock Exchange data with millisecond-level order-book access.
Alpha 潜力：

The study provides plausibly causal evidence that latency-induced HFT affects market quality, particularly in short-horizon price variation, adverse-selection exposure, and realized spreads. The findings can be used to design strategies that exploit latency differences or adapt to HFT-driven market conditions.
