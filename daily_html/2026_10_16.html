Twice Upon a Time: Complementary Representations for Time-Series Reasoning

时间序列推理中的互补表示：双重视角

Time Series Language Models (TSLMs) enable language-based understanding and reasoning over temporal data, yet a fundamental question remains: what information should their time-series representations preserve, and what variation should they factor out? Existing TSLMs typically expose the language model to a single representation, imposing a fixed inductive bias across downstream tasks. We instead introduce complementary representations as a design principle for time-series reasoning. Our model combines two visual views: line plots preserve absolute measurements and the observed coordinate frame, while normalized delay embeddings factor out positive amplitude rescaling and offsets while exposing temporal structure. Dedicated visual encoders integrate both views into a shared language model through visual tokens and multi-level feature fusion. Evaluation-time interventions verify that the two views exhibit distinct invariance profiles, while single-view ablations show that their combination is particularly beneficial beyond basic perception, with the large gains in prediction and reasoning. Built on Qwen3-VL-8B, our dual-view model improves overall TSRBench accuracy from 44.5% with line plots alone to 53.2%, substantially exceeding the strongest evaluated dedicated TSLM baseline at 36.7%. It further achieves 89.9% accuracy on TSExam and 86.8% numerical accuracy on multivariate ChatTS alignment. Together, these results demonstrate that complementary representations provide an effective design principle for general-purpose TSLMs, enabling models to exploit temporal structure without sacrificing access to the original measurement frame.

Where Backtests and Live Trading Part Ways: Six Defects from One Intraday System

回测与实盘分道扬镳之处：一个日内交易系统的六种缺陷

Backtests of intraday strategies can drift away from what the live system actually does; in our case, the drift never showed in summary statistics. We report six defects found while running one intraday US equity system live and comparing its live days with their backtest replay. Three came from data: a default feed that carried a small fraction of real opening volume, historical prices adjusted for dividends that live orders never see, and data the live system downloaded but the backtest never did. Two came from code: a sizing step that exceeded a limit the live account enforces, and UTC timestamps read as New York time. One came from configuration: settings that were switched on but never reached the code that places orders. For each we give the symptom, the size of the error, the cause and the test or control that now guards against it. All six sat where two paths that should agree had drifted apart. We close with a ten-point checklist. We make no claim about whether the system is profitable.

核心方法： Comparative analysis between backtest results and live trading performance to identify discrepancies, specifically focusing on data, code, and configuration defects in intraday US equity trading.
数据来源： Intraday US equity market data, including real-time feed data and historical prices adjusted for dividends.

How Much Screening Buys a Second? Speed, Selection, and the Maker's Spread

多少筛选能赢得一秒？速度、选择与做市商价差

We study the spread a liquidity provider needs to break even against counterparties that can exploit a stale quote, and we place two defenses on a single scale: repricing sooner and screening informed flow. A break-even condition pins down the exact screening rate at which a slower maker matches a faster unscreened venue: the selection that buys back the extra staleness. The result requires only an integrable price innovation and therefore holds under jumps, atoms, and infinite variance. Predictable drift is a centering problem: it widens a spot-centered quote, but its cost vanishes once the quote is recentered on the expected reference. In four USDC-WETH pools benchmarked against Binance ETH-USDT from June to August 2026, the one-second benchmark innovation is sharply non-Gaussian: 73% of seconds show no price change. Cutting cadence from twelve seconds to one sheds 84.5% of the expected shock, so a twelve-second maker must screen at least 84.5% of informed flow to match a one-second venue, far above the 71.1% that a Gaussian model implies. In trending markets, the stale pool is picked off at execution: with-trend fills obtain an effective spread 11.1 bps lower than against-trend fills, with the same sign in all four pools.

核心方法： The paper analyzes the break-even spread for liquidity providers by considering two key defenses: frequent repricing and screening of informed flow. It establishes a break-even condition that links the screening rate to the staleness of quotes, providing a quantitative framework applicable under various market conditions including jumps and infinite variance.

PathFM: Fast Generative Forecasting of Multivariate Trajectories

PathFM: 多变量轨迹的快速生成式预测

Time series foundation models are becoming increasingly popular for general-purpose forecasting. However, most leading models predict marginal quantiles, leaving the dependence structure of future trajectories unspecified. This distinction matters for trajectory-dependent quantities such as cumulative demand, peak load, and event timing, yet point and marginal scores do not reveal it. To model this dependence, we introduce PathFM, a trajectory-based forecasting model that uses flow matching to generate complete future paths. We further distill the model to one or two steps and share history attention cache across draws. Together, these enable PathFM to generate multiple paths for a single series in roughly the time needed for one query to a quantile model. The distilled sampler also makes it practical to optimize a calibration loss directly. PathFM achieves competitive marginal accuracy on the established forecasting benchmarks fev-bench and TIME. To complement these evaluations, we introduce Forecast Path Score Bench (fps-bench), which augments fev-bench tasks with energy and variogram scores and downstream metrics. PathFM achieves a higher aggregate path score on fps-bench than all evaluated external baselines. Experiments on chaotic systems further show that our flow model sustains long-run variability, while strong baselines rapidly collapse. PathFM makes generative time series forecasting both competitive and practical, enabling diverse downstream applications.

核心方法： Flow matching-based generative forecasting model that generates complete future trajectories (paths) for multivariate time series. Key innovations include: 1) Distillation to one or two steps for efficiency, 2) Shared history attention cache across draws, enabling faster multi-path generation than quantile models.
数据来源： Generic multivariate time series data (demonstrated on fev-bench and TIME forecasting benchmarks), potentially applicable to market data including: asset price series, order book dynamics, macroeconomic indicators.

Causal Integrated Gradients: Scalable Feature Attribution Along Causal Paths

因果积分梯度：沿因果路径的可扩展特征归因

Existing feature attribution methods that account for feature dependencies do not scale to high dimensions. We propose causal integrated gradients (CIG) and causal expected gradients (CEG), which extend integrated gradients to incorporate prior causal structure among input features while retaining its computational efficiency and, under conditions on the causal graph, its axiomatic guarantees. In many settings, some causal structure between features is already known, for example, that a transcription factor causally regulates a target gene's expression. Yet existing gradient-based methods cannot exploit this structure, instead crediting a feature's downstream effects over its true upstream cause, such as ranking the target gene above the transcription factor that regulates it. Our key idea is to embed this causal structure by routing the integration path along the causal graph. The method thereby decomposes each feature's attribution into a direct effect and effects mediated through its causal descendants. We evaluate CIG in three settings of increasing realism. First, on a synthetic benchmark with a known causal model, CIG's attributions best match the ground-truth causal effect from Pearl's do-calculus, matching the accuracy of causal Shapley values at a fraction of the computational cost. Second, on simulated single-cell gene expression data with a known gene regulatory network (Dyngen), CIG most accurately recovers the transcription factors driving the simulation. Third, on real interferon-$β$ stimulation data, where only an approximate causal graph is available, CIG better prioritizes the transcription factors driving the measured response than DeepSHAP and integrated gradients. Together, these results show that incorporating causal structure into gradient-based attributions recovers upstream drivers that existing methods miss, without sacrificing scalability.

核心方法： Causal Integrated Gradients (CIG) extends integrated gradients by incorporating prior causal structure among input features, routing the integration path along the causal graph to decompose each feature's attribution into direct and mediated effects.
数据来源： Gene expression data (simulated and real), including single-cell gene expression data with known gene regulatory networks and real interferon-$β$ stimulation data.
Alpha 潜力：

The method can be adapted to financial markets to identify upstream drivers of price movements or other market behaviors by leveraging known causal relationships, potentially uncovering hidden factors that traditional gradient-based methods miss.

SelectPFN: Amortized Bayesian Variable Selection for Sparse Nonlinear Regression with Prior-Data Fitted Networks

SelectPFN：基于先验数据拟合网络的稀疏非线性回归贝叶斯变量选择方法

Tabular foundation models based on prior-data fitted networks (PFNs) predict on a new data set in a single forward pass, but they cannot report which inputs their predictions depend on. We propose SelectPFN, a PFN that performs Bayesian variable selection and prediction jointly for sparse nonlinear regression. In one forward pass it returns posterior inclusion probabilities for every variable, and its predictions are computed from sampled subsets only, so excluded variables provably cannot affect them; we show this is exactly what the Bayesian model prescribes. The network is pretrained once on synthetic tasks from a meta-prior built around a group spike-and-slab sparse-input neural network. Column-permutation equivariance combined with random interleaving of original and knockoff columns yields knockoff statistics with exact finite-sample false discovery rate control, even when the meta-prior is wrong. We develop theory for amortized selection: a Bayes-regret bound for the amortized median probability model, a characterization showing that the information needed to detect a variable grows like $(2/λ)\log(1/ω)$ in the prior weight $ω$ of structures that can use it, where $λ\le1$ is an efficiency, and a negative result showing that average-case training does not transfer to frequentist guarantees at a fixed truth. On out-of-prior nonlinear designs with p up to 500, SelectPFN improves the area under the precision-recall curve by 0.23-0.28 over the lasso, BART, DART, LassoNet and a group-penalized sparse neural network, at a false discovery proportion of about 0.07 and 0.04 seconds per data set. The advantage persists on real covariate matrices, and, as the theory predicts, detection of interaction-only variables rises monotonically with their prior weight.

核心方法： The paper introduces SelectPFN, a prior-data fitted network that performs Bayesian variable selection and prediction jointly for sparse nonlinear regression. Risk-sensitive reinforcement learning (RL) addresses decision-making under uncertainty and adverse outcomes. Learning risk-sensitive policies from fixed datasets is important when additional interaction with the environment is costly. Existing work on risk-sensitive RL focuses on online settings or specific risk measures, leaving a general theoretical understanding of its offline setting underdeveloped. We consider two formulations: dynamic-OCE RL, which applies OCE recursively at each step, and static-OCE RL, which applies a single OCE to the cumulative rewards. We establish utility-dependent sublinear guarantees with explicit dependence on the feature dimension $d$. To our knowledge, we provide the first offline algorithms along with the first upper and lower suboptimality bounds for both dynamic- and static-OCE RL.

A fixed discount factor in Q-learning imposes a single planning horizon throughout training, despite the differing demands of early exploration and long-term credit assignment. We propose Q($γ_H$), a multi-horizon Q-learning framework that maintains multiple discount-specific heads and adaptively combines them at control time using an entropy-derived confidence signal. In-Context Preference-Conditioned Pareto Set Learning from Limited Offline Data

Multi-objective optimization involves diverse preferences over conflicting objectives. Rather than returning a finite set of trade-off solutions, preference-conditioned Pareto set learning seeks to map a specified preference directly to a corresponding Pareto optimal solution. Yet in many real-world scenarios, optimization must proceed offline without further objective evaluations, leaving only a limited static dataset that sparsely covers the decision and objective spaces. Existing methods either optimize separately for each preference or train a problem-specific Pareto solution model, requiring repeated search across preferences or repeated training across problems. We introduce in-context preference-conditioned Pareto set learning (ICPSL), a framework that amortizes both preference-wise solution search and problem-specific model construction. The generated model can then be reused to predict solutions for arbitrary preferences without problem-specific training or preference-wise search. Experiments on synthetic and real-world problems show that ICPSL produces high-quality Pareto solutions while providing substantial speedups in problem-specific solution-model construction.

Financial NLP benchmarks can therefore suffer from a within-event timing mismatch when the prediction target begins before the textual or audio input used by a model could be complete. Together, the results show that labels and losses can depend materially on the declared outcome clock even when model rankings appear stable. The Availability Contract makes this assumption explicit and ties post-input claims to the available timing evidence.

SkillNEO: Non-Elitist Evolutionary Optimization of Agent Skills

Agent skills turn procedural knowledge into reusable artifacts, allowing LLM agents to accumulate and refine expertise through experience. Building on this, skill evolution iteratively improves skills using execution feedback. However, improvement may require several interdependent edits, so low-reward versions can still be worth developing. Requiring immediate reward gains can discard useful partial repairs before their benefits emerge.
