FinLLM-KG: Knowledge Graph-Enhanced LLMs for Financial Sentiment Analysis in Algorithmic Trading

FinLLM-KG：利用知识图增强的LLM进行算法交易中的金融情感分析

Recent advances in Generative AI have substantially improved financial sentiment analysis through post-trained financial large language models (LLMs). However, existing approaches remain confined to predicting the sentiment of each article in isolation and operate as black boxes, which limits contextual understanding and hinders deployment in high-stakes financial decision-making. To this end, we introduce FinLLM-KG, a model-agnostic framework that combines causal LLMs with a financial knowledge graph, in order to inject temporally grounded, company-level knowledge from SEC 10-K filings at inference time, without updating model weights. Within FinLLM-KG, context is supplied either as a natural-language company profile or as a small set of relevance-ranked factual triples. This enables sentiment predictions to account for broader firm-level information rather than the article alone, at almost zero computational cost and for any causal LLM. In addition, attributing each article-level sentiment score to individual sentences and to the retrieved knowledge-graph facts makes the evidence behind the prediction inspectable, thereby enhancing explainability and supporting human-in-the-loop review. Simulations demonstrate that FinLLM-KG consistently improves portfolio performance over the state-of-the-art baseline.

The framework injects temporally grounded, company-level knowledge from SEC 10-K filings at inference time without updating model weights, improving contextual understanding and explainability.

FIDES-Live: Evaluating Behavioral Concordance in Sequential Trading Agents

FIDES-Live：评估顺序交易代理中的行为一致性

Sequential trading agents produce decisions, orders, execution receipts, account states, and reports that need not agree, even when the final portfolio value is correctly computed. We present FIDES-Live, an operational extension of FIDES from generated strategy concordance to the audit of sequential agent traces. The protocol separates structured decision validity, optional numerical commitment adherence, execution and ledger reconciliation, and receipt- grounded reporting, with explicit coverage and unscorable outcomes. We retrospectively audit ten requested inference routes over 7,299 cryptocurrency account-rounds and a 310-round equity pilot, using real market observations and local paper execution. Cryptocurrency decision validity is 97.35% and structured reporting accuracy is 90.81% among scorable responses, but route-level decision and reporting performance can diverge sharply. Commitment adherence is 94.36% overall; it falls to 62.63% on constraints emitted with nonempty order proposals, compared with 97.56% for valid empty proposals. All retained cash and position transitions reconcile with recorded fills, while execution controls and unavailable quotes explain separate rejection populations. Short-window marked returns provide descriptive context rather than a new test of whether concordance predicts alpha. These observations motivate reporting concordance, evidence coverage, activity, and financial outcomes separately. They do not establish long-run trading skill, semantic faithfulness of explanations, or causal benefits from audit feedback.

核心方法： FIDES-Live is an operational protocol designed to audit sequential trading agents by evaluating behavioral concordance across decision-making, execution, and reporting processes. It separates structured decision validity, numerical commitment adherence, execution and ledger reconciliation, and receipt-grounded reporting with explicit coverage and unscorable outcomes.
数据来源： The study utilizes real market observations and local paper execution across 7,299 cryptocurrency account-rounds and a 310-round equity pilot.

ATEMPO: Amortized Transfer Entropy Model Per-lag Over-Time

ATEMPO：跨时滞的摊销传递熵模型

When one time series informs another, the delay at which that information arrives determines if models can utilize this relationship effectively. A source may be strongly predictive at one delay but uninformative at neighboring lags. Therefore, fixed input windows may obscure this structure by bundling informative and irrelevant history together. Transfer entropy (TE) can recover these delays by measuring how much a source's past tells us about a target beyond the target's own history. However, existing TE estimators require separate fits across source and conditioning depths, with further estimation on local windows to track delays over time. We introduce ATEMPO, a diffusion-based transfer entropy estimator amortized over lags and time. ATEMPO samples source and conditioning depths during training and conditions explicitly on temporal position, allowing a single score network to estimate the full TE surface without refitting. Differencing this surface recovers when information arrives. Across synthetic datasets with ground-truth TE, ATEMPO achieves the lowest mean relative error among baselines, and on datasets with known lags, ATEMPO achieves $0.98\pm0.04$ lag-detection accuracy. ATEMPO requires a single fit across source depths $K=6$ and target depths $L=3$, whereas existing estimators require 18 separate fits to evaluate the same $K \times L$ grid. On three systems with time-varying lag, ATEMPO is the only estimator to maintain perfect lag-detection accuracy across all evaluated window lengths. These recovered delays improve downstream forecasting: ATEMPO-selected inputs reduce MSE on WEATHER-5K by 7.25%, while 120 selected inputs outperform the benchmark's 240-feature default by 4.6%. Across three brain-computer interface tasks, using ATEMPO-selected lags achieves the highest or equal-best $R^2$, improving mean $R^2$ by 4.1% relative to the strongest competing baseline.

核心方法： ATEMPO introduces a diffusion-based transfer entropy estimator that is amortized over lags and time, allowing for efficient estimation of the full TE surface without requiring separate fits for different source and conditioning depths. This method samples source and conditioning depths during training and conditions explicitly on temporal position.
数据来源： Synthetic datasets with ground-truth TE, weather data (WEATHER-5K), and brain-computer interface tasks.
Alpha 潜力：

The method's ability to accurately detect information delays and improve forecasting performance suggests potential for identifying leading indicators in financial time series, which could be exploited for predictive trading strategies.

Learning from Points and Patches: A Pre-trained Hybrid Attention Transformer for Time Series Analysis

从点和块中学习：一种用于时间序列分析的预训练混合注意力Transformer

Time-series data generated by Web services and connected systems support applications such as user activity analysis, service monitoring, and resource management. However, learning transferable representations from heterogeneous time series remains challenging due to diverse temporal patterns, variable sequence lengths, and limited high-quality annotations. Despite the success of pre-training in natural language processing and computer vision, effective time-series pre-training requires strategies tailored to temporal data. We propose TimeHAT (Hybrid Attention Transformer), a general-purpose pre-training framework for time-series analysis that addresses two complementary questions: what to learn and how to learn. For what to learn, we introduce a Unified Point-Patch Masking Strategy (UPPMS) that integrates point-level and patch-level supervision within a unified framework for contextual reconstruction and causal extrapolation, enabling the model to capture both fine-grained temporal variations and broader temporal patterns. For how to learn, we develop a Hybrid Attention Encoder (HAE) that combines prefix-constrained bidirectional attention in initial layers with directional hybrid attention in deeper layers, aiming to preserve effective contextual interactions while mitigating rank degeneration. We evaluate TimeHAT across real-world datasets and four downstream tasks: classification, long-term forecasting, imputation, and anomaly detection. Experiments cover fine-tuning, few-shot, and zero-shot transfer settings, demonstrating competitive performance across diverse time-series analysis tasks. Code is available at https://github.com/TangPeiwang/TimeHAT.

LiveSearch: Questions as Executable Verifier Code for a Live Benchmark of Professional-Source Financial Search Agents

LiveSearch：将问题作为可执行验证代码，用于专业金融搜索代理的实时基准测试

In finance, the answer that matters is usually the current one: prices, rates, and disclosures hold only as of a given day, so retrieving up-to-date facts is a core capability of a financial agent. Yet most financial benchmarks overlook timeliness. They fix each answer at annotation time, so the gold goes stale as markets move and, once leaked into training data, rewards recall rather than search and analysis. We introduce LiveSearch, a live benchmark that stores each question as executable verifier code: a typed program of calls to a professional financial data service, followed by a declarative extractor. The question is frozen, while its gold answer is determined by executing the program against professional financial data at evaluation time, and a daily job re-executes it with no human or language model in the loop. A memorized answer is thus likely outdated, and a model must reach the current one through tools and analysis. Construction is equally automatic: a language model only verbalizes programs enumerated from real tool responses and never chooses an answer. Under general web search, six contemporary tool-calling models reach 70.9% to 76.7% answer accuracy. Over three consecutive days, 70% of served questions change their gold value, and even under tolerance-aware matching, a literally reused earlier snapshot fails on a majority of questions within a single day. Per-question tracking uncovers dynamic shifts in correctness masked by aggregate accuracy.

核心方法： LiveSearch introduces a live benchmark for financial search agents by storing questions as executable verifier code that dynamically retrieves current financial data from professional services. This ensures answers are always up-to-date, requiring models to use tools and analysis rather than relying on memorized data.
数据来源： Professional financial data services providing real-time prices, rates, and disclosures.
Alpha 潜力：

Statistical anomaly detection methods like Isolation Forest are efficient but suffer from a critical semantic gap. To bridge this gap, we propose ReForest, a novel framework that synergizes the statistical efficiency of Isolation Forest with the reasoning power of LLMs. ReForest transforms the latent decision paths of Isolation Forest into natural-language explanations, enabling an LLM to refine anomaly detection through an uncertainty-aware dynamic ensemble. Extensive experiments on four real-world datasets (Healthcare, Finance) demonstrate that ReForest significantly outperforms baselines, achieving an average 21% improvement in Recall and a 10% gain in F1-score. Our framework not only improves detection performance but also enhances model transparency by grounding semantic narratives in statistical evidence, providing interpretable rationales essential for high-stakes decision-making.

TRIDENT-Seq：基于行为匹配的前缀-未来对比的低标签支付风险建模

Ant International accumulates large volumes of transaction history across users and payment businesses. Confirmed fraud and chargeback outcomes, by contrast, are scarce, delayed, and specific to each business. A single encoder pretrained on unlabeled histories could serve all businesses, but only if pretraining captures the behavior that separates risk. Coarse activity differences can make contrastive pretraining on transaction logs too easy: transaction volume, amount, and timing can distinguish a history's continuation from arbitrary candidates without modeling how payment resources are used. We present TRIDENT-Seq, a transaction foundation model trained with Prefix-Future Contrast (PFC). PFC matches each prefix to a summary of its subsequent behavior among candidates with similar activity profiles; activity prediction and dispersion regularization act on the encoder output. Across three production payment businesses, the pretrained encoder improves average precision (AP) and recall at a 0.5% intervention budget over architecture-matched scratch training in all six business-budget settings. With a fixed validation set, it exceeds full-label scratch training in mean AP and recall using 10% of the training labels on Wallet and 25% on Shopping and SME. Pretraining only on another business's histories still helps. Replacing profile-matched candidates with random ones cuts recall from 24.62% to 5.64%. In Ant International's deployment evaluation, recorded amount-based chargeback and overall loss rates are 37.7% and 37.9% lower than those of the previous XGBoost-based system under the same intervention budget.

核心方法： TRIDENT-Seq uses a transaction foundation model trained with Prefix-Future Contrast (PFC). PFC matches each historical transaction prefix to a summary of its future behavior among similar activity profiles, incorporating activity prediction and dispersion regularization. This approach ensures the model captures nuanced risk behaviors beyond simple transaction volume or timing differences.
数据来源： Large volumes of transaction histories from multiple payment businesses, focusing on user transaction logs including volume, amount, and timing data.

Test-Time Communication in Trading Agent Swarms

交易代理群体中的测试时通讯研究

Communication between agents has mostly been studied on tasks with a shared objective and fast, clear feedback. Trading has neither: each agent manages its own account, with decisions being scored by markets late and noisily. A trading swarm is thus a population of independent decision-makers rather than a team, for which convergence is a risk and not the goal. We ran 200 agents from four model families in groups of ten under five communication regimes, from isolation to message boards and shared notes, over seven US trading days (1 to 9 October 2026), logging everything each agent read before every decision. In two of the four families, communicating groups came to hold far more of the same stocks than the isolated group. A group's return was largely the return on what its members held in common. Seeing a post from a groupmate made an agent 7 percentage points more likely to reuse an eight-word phrase from it. Agents that started too close together to see each other's posts shared such a phrase in 14% of cases, the rate that common inputs produce. Seeing the post did not detectably change that morning's trades. Case studies traced the adoption of positions and decision rules over subsequent days, and communicating groups sold in step more often than isolated ones. Because isolated groups also converged on the same stocks, sometimes completely, assessments of herding need to measure excess convergence relative to a no-peer baseline.

核心方法： Experimental study of communication effects in trading agent swarms under different communication regimes (isolation, message boards, shared notes) with detailed logging of agent decisions and interactions.
数据来源： US stock market data (1 to 9 October 2026) with full agent decision logs including all messages read before trading decisions.
Alpha 潜力：

Identifies herding behavior and information diffusion patterns in agent populations, which could be exploited for front-running or contrarian strategies against retail trading swarms. Strategyproof Deep Reinforcement Learning for Online Resource Allocation

面向在线资源分配的策略不可欺骗深度强化学习

Online resource allocation faces two coupled challenges: (1) allocating scarce resources efficiently under uncertain future demand, and (2) ensuring that agents report task valuations and requirements truthfully (strategyproofness). Reinforcement Learning (RL) adapts allocation decisions through experience but does not necessarily ensure strategyproofness. Existing strategyproof RL uses a linear allocation rule, which limits the resource interactions it can represent. Neural mechanism design methods offer greater expressiveness, but provide only approximate incentive guarantees. Against this background, we introduce the first strategyproof deep RL mechanism for online resource allocation. It integrates three novel components: (i) a monotonic neural network that learns nonlinear interactions by construction (higher reported value cannot reduce acceptance, while a longer reported duration or larger resource requirement cannot increase it); (ii) a generalised critical payment: the lowest accepted value, with all other inputs fixed; and (iii) training by either RL or imitation of an existing rule. Together, monotonicity and the critical payment ensure strategyproofness and individual rationality, while the neural rule provides flexibility and imitation improves training efficiency. Empirically, on a four-resource stream with two partner pairs that a linear difference cannot represent, the neural rule achieves 97.8% of an offline welfare upper bound, compared with 93.8% for a linear learner and 89.0% for first-come, first-served, closing 65% and 80% of the remaining optimality gap, respectively. Moreover, imitation reaches 97.5% in only 261 seconds, about 89 times faster than RL.

核心方法： The paper introduces a strategyproof deep reinforcement learning mechanism for online resource allocation, featuring three novel components: a monotonic neural network for learning nonlinear interactions, a generalized critical payment mechanism, and training through RL or imitation learning. This approach ensures strategyproofness and individual rationality while maintaining flexibility and efficiency in resource allocation.

Timestral

Timestral

We introduce Timestral, a family of four time series foundation models for probabilistic forecasting, with 5M, 28M, 123M, and 209M parameters. Timestral sets a new state of the art on GIFT-Eval, TIME, and fev-bench, where Timestral-209M and Timestral-123M take the top two places on every metric. We achieve this with three ingredients: Decoupled Horizon Forecasting (DHF), which separates prediction tokens from the causally encoded context, so that longer horizons are trained without masking context and each observed value is both a forecast target and context for later forecasts; a covariate framework that natively supports known-future covariates within a causal architecture; and a two-stage pipeline that pretrains exclusively on a diverse, large-scale synthetic corpus before post-training on a mix of real and synthetic data with extended context. This report describes the model design and its benchmark results. Weights for all four models are available on Hugging Face and inference code on GitHub.

核心方法： The paper introduces Timestral, a family of time series foundation models for probabilistic forecasting, featuring Decoupled Horizon Forecasting (DHF), a covariate framework for known-future covariates, and a two-stage training pipeline involving synthetic and real data.
数据来源： The model uses a diverse, large-scale synthetic corpus for pretraining, followed by post-training on a mix of real and synthetic data.
